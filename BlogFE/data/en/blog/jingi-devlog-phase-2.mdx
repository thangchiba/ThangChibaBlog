## Making the prototype easier to use

For Beta 0.2.0, I worked on the parts around combat: managing items, showing character health, and moving between scenes.

<YouTubeEmbed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mkFDlAXRNb4&t=48s" />

## Inventory and GUI

The inventory supports dragging items, equipping them, and using them. Tooltips show what each item does. I used assets from indie mobile games and wrote the scripts needed to fit them into Jingi.

I also updated the health bar and loading scenes. These are small changes, but they make the prototype easier to follow while playing.

## Pets, for now

Pets can follow their owner around. They cannot attack yet; that will need a separate set of scripts.

## Preparing for networking

Networking is the next big task. At this point, I expect to rewrite roughly 70% of the code to make it fit multiplayer. That is an estimate of the work ahead, rather than a finished migration. Some of the current systems are likely to change along the way.
