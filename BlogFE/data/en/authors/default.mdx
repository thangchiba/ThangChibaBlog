## Hi, I'm Thang <Twemoji emoji="waving hand" />

I'm a software engineer from Vietnam, living in Chiba and working in Japan. At work I build business applications, AI tools, and infrastructure. In my own time, I like making games and small apps for people around me.

I can get so absorbed in coding that I set an alarm to remind myself to go to bed. C# is my favorite language, though I use Java, React, and Python regularly too.

## What matters to me when building software

I want software to make a task easier for the person using it. That means thinking about UX even when most of my work is in the backend.

I try to write code that another person can follow, and to learn through working examples. Before proposing an unfamiliar technology at work, I usually test it in a personal project. It helps me understand where it is useful and where it adds complexity.

## My work so far

I started working as a software engineer in Japan in April 2021. Early projects included E-Commerce, warehouse management, and internal applications. From April 2022 to March 2025, I worked on digitizing a B2B wholesaler's workflows.

In 2025, I led work on an AI Agent platform and an E-Commerce agent, then joined an SRE team working on a Data Lake module. You can find the timeline and details in [my resume](/resume).

## Why I write here

This blog is a place to record what I tried, what went wrong, and what I learned. Writing it down helps me remember, and I hope some of it is useful to others building similar things.

My longer-term personal goal is to build a shared virtual world. Projects such as [Jingi](/project/jingi), [NeoSocket](/project/neo-socket), and [NeoSekai](/project/neo-sekai) are how I explore the gameplay and networking questions along the way.
